# /brief — Morning Brief Generator

> Generate your daily brief. Tell me where you're at and I'll help you fill it in.

## Usage

`/brief` or just describe your status in plain English

## What It Does

Reads your `context/daily-brief.md` template and helps you fill it in by asking 3 quick questions:
1. What's your energy/bandwidth today?
2. What's the one most important thing for Resob.ai today?
3. Any blockers or urgent things?

Then generates a clean, filled-in daily brief you can act on.

## Context It Uses

- `context/daily-brief.md` — template
- `context/weekly-review.md` — current week status
- `context/goals-dashboard.md` — active goals
- `context/current-data.md` — latest metrics
- `context/task-audit.md` — automation priorities

## Tips

- Run this FIRST THING in the morning before checking messages or social media
- Takes 3-5 minutes max
- The brief keeps you aligned — without it, you wake up and react instead of act
- If you skip a day, don't skip two. The rhythm is what matters.
