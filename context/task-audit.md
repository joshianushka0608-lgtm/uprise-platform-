## Key Insight from This Audit

**Your biggest problem isn't that you lack tasks — it's that nothing has a system.**

Almost every task you do is:
- Inconsistent (sometimes, randomly)
- In your head (not tracked anywhere)
- Reactive (you do it when you think of it)

**The fix isn't automating everything — it's systematizing first, then automating.**

Building systems BEFORE automating is why most people fail at this. The Quick Wins above are all about creating structure so things don't depend on your memory or willpower.
