**Priority Order:**

|---|---|---|---|

**Current Affairs Focus Areas:**
- Schemes launched by Central/State govt
- Appointments (President, PM, CJI, RBI Governor)
- International summits (G20, BRICS, NATO, etc.)
- Major ports, dams, national parks
- Awards (Nobel, Padma, Nobel in Literature/Science)

**Most Repeated History Topics:**
- Ancient: Indus Valley, Vedic Period, Maurya/Gupta Empire, Buddhism & Jainism
- Medieval: Delhi Sultanate, Mughal Empire, Sufi & Bhakti movement
- Modern: 1857 Revolt, Congress sessions, Freedom movement leaders

**Most Repeated Polity Topics:**
- Parts of Constitution (Preamble, DPSP, Fundamental Rights)
- Schedules (1-12)
- Amendments
- Parliament structure

## MONTH-WISE SMART STUDY PLAN (6 Weeks Sprint)

### Week 1-2: CRACK THE EASY MARKS
- **Quant:** Simplification, Percentage, Ratio, Average, SI/CI
- **Reasoning:** Series, Analogy, Coding-Decoding, Directions, Blood Relations
- **English:** Error Spotting, Fill in Blanks, Synonyms, Idioms
- **GA:** Current Affairs (last 6 months notes)

### Week 3-4: MODERATE TOPICS
- **Quant:** Geometry, Trigonometry, Algebra, Time & Work
- **Reasoning:** Puzzles, Syllogism, Statement-Arguments
- **English:** Cloze Test, RC, Para Jumbles
- **GA:** History, Polity (focus NCERT + PYQs)

### Week 5: DATA & INTEGRATION
- **Quant:** DI (Bar + Pie + Tabular)
- **Reasoning:** Venn Diagram, Figure-based
- **English:** Active-Passive, Direct-Indirect, Spelling
- **GA:** Geography, Science, Static GK

### Week 6: MOCK MODE
- 1 mock test every 2 days
- Review wrong answers — don't just count score
- Focus weak areas flagged by mock analysis

## PYQ (Previous Year Questions) STRATEGY

**Most Repeated Questions — Practice These First:**
1. From Simplification: BODMAS, fraction-to-decimal conversions
2. From Percentage: percentage to fraction/destination values
3. From Geometry: area of triangle given sides, circle theorems
4. From Trigonometry: value of sin/cos at standard angles
5. From Series: next term in pattern series
6. From Cloze: 2022-2025 CGL cloze passages
7. From Current Affairs: Jan-Dec major events of last 6 months
