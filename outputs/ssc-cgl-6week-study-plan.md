# SSC CGL — 6-Week Sprint Plan
**Created:** 2026-04-22 | **Exam Target:** TBD (aim for 6 weeks)  
**Focus:** Math (highest) + Reasoning (already done basics) + English (light prep) + Basic GA only

---

## Your Starting Point
- ✅ Reasoning: Basics done — just need to sharpen and add speed
- ⚠️ English: Some done — focus on高分 areas (Cloze, RC, Error Spotting)
- 🔴 Math: Fresh — highest ROI, most ground to cover
- 🔴 GA: Only basic static GK — skip deep study, just cover high-yield

```

10:00–10:30 AM →  BREAK

10:30–12:00 PM →  Reasoning (90 min) [Speed building + weak areas]

Evening (Light)
─────────────────────────────────────────────
6:00–6:45 PM   →  GA (45 min) [Current affairs + static GK — passive]
```

> **Total daily study: ~5.5–6 hours**  
> Rest on Sunday — no study, full recharge.

## The 6-Week Breakdown

### Week 1 — Build the Foundation (April 22–28)
> **Theme:** Easy marks first. Get the low-hanging fruit locked.

**Math (Mon/Wed/Fri/Sat):**
- Day 1: Simplification BODMAS rules + 50 PYQs
- Day 2: Percentage (basics + PYQs 2018-2023)
- Day 3: Ratio & Proportion + Mixture-Alligation
- Day 4: Average + Number System basics
- Day 5: Speed, Distance & Time basics
- Day 6: 100 Mixed Math PYQs (timer on)
- Day 7: REST

**English (Tue/Thu/Sat):**
- Day 1: Cloze Test — technique + 30 PYQs
- Day 2: Error Spotting — rules + 30 PYQs
- Day 3: Reading Comprehension — 3 passages PYQs
- Day 4: Fill in the Blanks (Grammar + Vocab)
- Day 5: Synonyms/Antonyms —高频词 list
- Day 6: 50 Mixed English PYQs
- Day 7: REST

**Reasoning (Daily 1.5 hrs):**
- Days 1-5: Focus one topic per day
  - Day 1: Series (missing number + alphabet series)
  - Day 2: Analogy + Classification
  - Day 3: Coding-Decoding (letter + number based)
  - Day 4: Blood Relations + Direction Sense
  - Day 5: Syllogism + Statement-Arguments
- Day 6: 50 Reasoning PYQs (mixed, timed)
- Day 7: REST

**GA (Evening 45 min daily):**
- Current Affairs: Read last 3 months (Jan–Mar 2026) — 15 min/day
- Static GK: Indian History (ancient + medieval) — 30 min/day

**Weekly Target:**
- Math: 150 PYQs solved + concepts locked
- English: 100 PYQs solved + 1 technique mastered per topic
- Reasoning: 100 PYQs + weak areas identified
- GA: 3 months current affairs done, History basics covered

### Week 5 — Speed + Mocks (May 20–26)
> **Theme:** Time management. You need speed now.

**Every day:**
- 1 Mock (any section — alternate Math/English/Reasoning)
- After each mock: 30 min review (what went wrong)
- Weak topic practice (15-20 min based on errors)

**Schedule:**
- Mon: Math Mock + review
- Tue: English Mock + review
- Wed: Reasoning Mock + review
- Thu: Math Mock + review
- Fri: English Mock + review
- Sat: Full Tier 1 Mock (all 4 sections, timed)
- Sun: Deep review of Saturday mock + GA revision

**GA:** Finish current affairs (last 2 months) + static GK quick revision

### Week 6 — Exam Mode (May 27 – June 1)
> **Theme:** Peak performance. Light study, max confidence.

**Mon:** 1 full mock (all sections, exact exam conditions)

**Thu:** 1 full mock (last year's paper)
**Fri:** Final formula sheet review + current affairs last look
**Sat (Exam day):** Light morning review → go in confident

## Key Reminders

1. **Math is your highest-leverage.** Every hour in Math = more marks than any other section.
2. **PYQs are gold.** 15-20 questions repeat. If you solve 2018-2025 PYQs, you're guaranteed to see familiar questions.
3. **Speed matters.** Start timing yourself from Week 3 onwards. 25 Math Qs in 25 min, 25 English in 20 min.
4. **No new topics in Week 6.** Just revise what you know.
5. **GA is low ROI.** Spend 45 min/day max. Don't fall into the GA rabbit hole.
